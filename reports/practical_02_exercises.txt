**Exercise F: Duplicate Rows and Repeated Timestamps**

A duplicate entire row occurs when all values in a row are exactly the same as another row.

A repeated timestamp occurs when two or more rows have the same date and time, but other column values may be different.

The `df.duplicated().sum()` command checks completely duplicate rows. Our dataset has 0 duplicate rows.
